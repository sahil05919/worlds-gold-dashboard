Where Is the Physical Gold?

Who owns gold and where that gold is stored are not always the same thing.

Sovereign central banks frequently hold physical gold abroad in major international financial vaults (like London or New York) to facilitate trade settlement, emergency liquidity, and international custody.

Physical Gold vs. Financial Gold Exposure

Understanding the difference between holding metal directly and holding financial contracts.

PHYSICAL GOLD
  • Forms: Jewellery, Sovereign Coins, Bullion Bars, Central Bank Ingot Stock.
  • Ownership: Direct physical possession or allocated vault ownership.
  • Counterparty Risk: Zero credit risk (the metal itself is the asset).
FINANCIAL GOLD EXPOSURE
  • Forms: Gold ETFs (e.g., GLD, IAU), Futures Contracts, Mining Equities.
  • Ownership: You hold shares or paper contracts representing a financial claim.
  • Physical Link: Reputable ETFs hold physical bars in custodian vaults on behalf of shareholders.
If I buy a Gold ETF online, do I personally own a gold bar?

No. When you buy shares in a physically backed Gold ETF, you own a share of a trust. The trust holds allocated physical gold bars inside a custodian vault (such as the Bank of England or HSBC London). You do not personally take home a physical bar, but your share value directly tracks the price of gold stored in the vault.

Is all central-bank gold stored inside its own country?

No. Many sovereign nations store a portion of their official reserves abroad in cities like London or New York. This historical practice provides rapid access to global liquidity, enables instant trading settlement on international exchanges, and hedges against domestic geographical risks.

Why Does Gold's Price Move?

Gold's market price reflects a dynamic combination of four primary economic drivers:

1. INFLATION & FIAT CURRENCY

Purchasing Power Concerns

As paper currencies lose purchasing power to inflation, investors seek hard assets that preserve real wealth.

2. INTEREST RATES

Opportunity Cost

Because gold yields no interest income, lower real interest rates reduce the opportunity cost of holding physical metal.

3. CENTRAL BANK DEMAND

Sovereign Accumulation

Central bank net purchases (over 1,000t/year) create sustained structural demand on global exchanges.

4. UNCERTAINTY & RISK

Geopolitical Safe Haven

During geopolitical friction or banking system stress, capital flows toward safe-haven assets with zero counterparty risk.

How Much New Gold Is Mined Each Year?

Annual Mine Production: ~3,650 Tonnes / Year

Surprising Gold Facts

8 verified facts about the element gold

FACT 01

Indestructible Element

Gold is chemical element 79. It does not tarnish, corrode, or decay over millennia.

FACT 02

Extreme Density

Gold weighs 19.32 grams per cm³. A standard 1-liter bottle filled with gold would weigh 19.32 kg (42.6 lbs).

FACT 03

Infinitely Recyclable

Gold can be melted down and reminted repeatedly without losing any purity or metallic properties.

FACT 04

Essential for Technology

Because it is highly conductive and corrosion-free, gold is used in smartphone circuits, space satellites, and medical implants.

FACT 05

Sovereign Reserve Asset

Central banks hold 38,666 tonnes of gold as ultimate monetary backing that carries no credit or counterparty risk.

FACT 06

Thousands of Years of Money

Gold has functioned as a universal store of value and currency anchor across civilizations for over 4,000 years.

FACT 07

Jewellery Dominance

Personal jewellery accounts for ~44.4% of all above-ground gold stock, primarily in India and China.

FACT 08

Geographically Mined

Gold is extracted on every continent except Antarctica. Top modern mining nations include China, Australia, Russia, and the USA.
